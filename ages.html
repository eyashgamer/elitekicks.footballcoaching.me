Programme Structure

Four age groups,
one clear path.

Players move through four stages as they grow — each with its own focus, group size and coach.

U7

Ages 4–7 · First Touches

Ball familiarity, coordination and simple games. The goal at this stage is confidence and enjoyment, not results.

U12

Ages 8–12 · Fundamentals

Passing, dribbling and positional basics, plus the first real introduction to playing as a team.

U14

Ages 13–14 · Tactical Development

Structured formations, decision-making under pressure and a step up in training intensity.

U17

Ages 15–17 · Competitive Play

Match-focused training, physical conditioning, and preparation for trials or higher-level football.
